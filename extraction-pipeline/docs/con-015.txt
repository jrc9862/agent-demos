SERVICES AGREEMENT
1. This Agreement between Meridian Works and Tallgrass Co is effective as of 13 August, 2024.
2. The initial term is twelve (12) months. The term renews automatically for successive twelve (12) month periods unless cancelled.
3. Either party may terminate on 30 days written notice. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York.
4. Invoices are due within fifteen (15) days of receipt.
